## Initiative Overview

Recognizing that medical debt is the leading cause of rural and middle-class household financial distress in India, **MRP Associates** organized its 4th Annual Community Health &amp; Insurance Literacy Camp.

Over the two-day weekend camp, more than **1,200 participants** received free vital health diagnostics from visiting specialist doctors, while our advisory desk reviewed existing policy documents to explain coverage gaps, waiting periods, and network hospital rights.

## Key Takeaways from Our Policy Audit Desk

During the camp, our analysis of over 450 attendee policies revealed eye-opening statistics:
- **68% of families** were insured for ₹3 Lakhs or less—far below the average ICU treatment cost for cardiac or multi-organ failure.
- **54% of policyholders** were unaware that their policies had a 1% room-rent cap, which would trigger massive proportionate deductions on doctor and OT fees.
- **Over 200 seniors** were guided on porting their existing policies to specialized senior-citizen floater covers with reduced pre-existing waiting periods.

## Impact &amp; Community Feedback

We distributed over 1,500 complimentary copies of our Tamil and English booklet: **"The Complete Handbook to Cashless Hospital Claims in Tamil Nadu"**, empowering patients to claim their rightful benefits without out-of-pocket delays.
