## The Million Dollar Round Table (MDRT) Legacy

Founded in 1927 in the United States, the **Million Dollar Round Table (MDRT)** is widely recognized as the global premier association of financial professionals. Qualification is granted solely to the world's most accomplished life insurance and financial services professionals who demonstrate exceptional professional knowledge, strict ethical conduct, and outstanding client service.

Qualifying for MDRT even once is a career defining milestone. Maintaining this qualification **consecutively for 17 years — from 2007 to the present day** (*Insurance முகவர்களுக்கான MDRT (USA) Qualifier விருது 2007 முதல் நடப்பு வரை 17 வருடங்களாக பெற்று வருகிறோம்*) — establishes MRP Associates among an elite circle of financial advisors worldwide.
