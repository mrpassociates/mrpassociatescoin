## Event Overview

In an era of volatile financial markets and pervasive social media financial noise, MRP Associates organized the **Karur Mega Investor Awareness Summit 2025**. The half-day conclave brought together over 500 professionals, textile entrepreneurs, doctors, teachers, and young investors under one roof.

The summit featured distinguished keynote speakers and senior fund managers from India's foremost Asset Management Companies, including **SBI Mutual Fund**, **HDFC AMC**, and **ICICI Prudential AMC**.

## Interactive Q&amp;A Session

The most celebrated portion of the summit was the 90-minute open-floor Q&amp;A session where attendees asked pressing questions on:
- How to rebalance mutual funds during market highs and drawdowns.
- Navigating the Old vs. New Tax Regime and claiming Section 80C and 80D deductions.
- Selecting pure term insurance versus high-premium endowment schemes.

Every attendee received a complimentary **"Financial Independence Blueprint Kit"** prepared by MRP Associates, detailing asset allocation guidelines and personal emergency fund calculators.
