## The Milestone Journey

In August 2025, **MRP Associates** achieved a landmark organizational milestone: guiding over **₹500 Crores ($60M+) in cumulative Assets Under Advice (AUA)** across retail equity mutual funds, hybrid investments, corporate debt, and structured retirement portfolios.

What started 15 years ago as a humble financial advisory desk in Karur has grown into a respected regional institution serving more than **10,000 families** throughout Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and NRI clients across the globe.

## Honoring Long-Standing Investors

At our private commemoration banquet, we honored 25 client families who have maintained uninterrupted SIP investments with MRP Associates for over 12 consecutive years. 

Each of these families started with modest monthly contributions (between ₹2,000 and ₹10,000) and saw their disciplined investments multiply into multi-crore retirement corpuses through market cycles, weathering the 2013 taper tantrum, 2016 demonetization, and 2020 pandemic recovery.
