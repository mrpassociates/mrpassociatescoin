## Specialized Life &amp; Pension Solutions

We specialize in tailor-made protection packages covering:

<div class="insurance-types">
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #2563eb;">
    <h4>Term Life Insurance</h4>
    <p>Pure risk protection offering high sum assured (₹1 Cr – ₹10 Cr+) at extremely affordable annual premiums. Critical for primary income earners.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #10b981;">
    <h4>Whole Life Insurance</h4>
    <p>Guaranteed lifetime protection up to age 99 or 100 with guaranteed death benefit and cash value accumulation for legacy planning.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #f59e0b;">
    <h4>Endowment &amp; Guaranteed Income</h4>
    <p>Combines life cover with guaranteed lump-sum maturity returns, making it ideal for risk-averse investors saving for specific life milestones.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #8b5cf6;">
    <h4>Unit Linked Insurance Plans (ULIPs)</h4>
    <p>Flexible dual-benefit plans offering market-linked equity and debt growth alongside life coverage, with tax-free fund switching under Section 10(10D).</p>
  </div>
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #ec4899;">
    <h4>Child Milestone &amp; Education Plans</h4>
    <p>Structured payouts matching critical college milestones (ages 18, 21, 24) with waiver-of-premium benefit if the parent passes away prematurely.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="insurance-type-card" style="border-left-color: #06b6d4;">
    <h4>Annuity &amp; Retirement Pension Plans</h4>
    <p>Accumulate a dedicated corpus during your working years and convert it into a guaranteed lifelong monthly pension with inflation options.</p>

## How Much Coverage Do You Need?

A sound rule of thumb is a **Sum Assured equal to 10 to 15 times your annual gross income**, plus your outstanding liabilities (loans) minus your liquid savings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<div class="faq-section">
  <div class="faq-item">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-circle-question" style="color: #2563eb;"></i> What is the right age to purchase term life insurance?</h4>
    <p>The younger you buy, the lower your premium locked for the entire tenure. For instance, a 25-year-old can secure ₹1 Crore term cover for as little as ₹600–₹800 per month, locked until retirement age.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="faq-item">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-circle-question" style="color: #2563eb;"></i> What happens if I survive the term insurance tenure?</h4>
    <p>Pure term insurance pays out upon demise during the term. If you prefer getting your money back, we also offer <strong>Term Return of Premium (TROP)</strong> plans where 100% of all premiums paid are refunded at maturity.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="faq-item">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-circle-question" style="color: #2563eb;"></i> Can I purchase multiple life insurance policies?</h4>
    <p>Yes. Many clients hold a base policy with LIC and an additional high-value term plan with private insurers like HDFC or ICICI Prudential. You must disclose all active covers during underwriting.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="faq-item">
    <h4><i class="fas fa-circle-question" style="color: #2563eb;"></i> How does MRP Associates help during an insurance claim?</h4>
    <p>During an unfortunate event, the nominee contacts us directly. We verify the paperwork, submit claims to the insurance headquarters, track underwriting approval, and ensure rapid direct credit to the nominee's account.</p>
  </div>
